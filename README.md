Power rankings, player tier lists, scouting analytics, and community content for amateur League of Legends draft leagues — live at **[leaguerankr.com](https://leaguerankr.com)**.

<img width="600" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1dd5182c-b732-4453-b61b-da5c38f806c7" />

## Features
- **Team Power Rankings**: Drag-and-drop weekly division rankings with per-team commentary, automatic edition-to-edition rank change tracking, customizable image overlays, and shareable PNG & Discord OpenGraph cards.
- **Player Tier Lists**: Overall and role-split tier lists across Standard and Draft Day presets, with customizable image overlays and PNG / OpenGraph exports.
- **Consensus Power Rankings**: Automated per-edition community averages across all published submissions with rank ranges, movement deltas, voting panel attribution, and PNG exports.
- **Community Blogs**: Markdown article editor with image uploads, layout controls, season tagging, and automatic URL/media sanitization.
- **Private Champion Pool Builder**: Client-side encrypted local champion pools protected by a user passphrase, complete with role catalogs and champion matchup lookups.
- **Stats Dashboard & Scouting Suite**: Role-normalized Rankr Rating, First Team All-Pro, customizable stat leaderboards, team standings, single- and double-elimination playoff brackets, division KPIs, and dedicated **Team**, **Player**, **Champion**, and **Match Series** scouting pages with draft/ban histories and Riot Solo/Duo mastery sync.
- **Multi-League Operations & Match Hub**: Per-league Operators, league/global creator allowlists, configurable season structures and schedules, CSV & Riot Match-V5 ingestion, and a Team Captain **Match Hub** combining upcoming schedules, opponent scouting, and Riot Tournament Code scaffolding.
- **Moderation & Publishing Controls**: Discord OAuth authentication, server-enforced content moderation rules, edition open/archive locks, and Discord publish webhook alerts.
